An OmniPlan alternative you buy once

Prices checked November 14, 2026

OmniPlan 4 is a full project suite for Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Apple Vision Pro. GanttOWN is a Gantt chart for Mac. You pay once, and the plan stays in a .gantt file on your machine.

The price difference

On Omni's buy page, OmniPlan 4 Standard is $199.99 once and Pro is $399.99 once. A Pro subscription is $199.99 a year. Those purchases cover Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Apple Vision Pro. Omni lists a 30-day money-back guarantee, and the Mac App Store listing includes a two-week trial.

GanttOWN is $49, $149, or $299 once. There is no renewal. Solo keeps bug and security fixes for the version you bought. Team and Business also include later features. The try-it path is a 14-day refund: email hello@ownsuite.io. There is no separate trial build.

Choose OmniPlan when the extra depth is the point

  • You level resources, or you need a network diagram.
  • The same plan has to open on an iPhone, an iPad, or Apple Vision Pro.
  • You already own OmniPlan 4 and the upgrade discount applies.

Choose GanttOWN when the schedule is the job

  • Phases that roll up progress, and collapse with one click.
  • Dependencies with lag in working days. Late work pushes everything after it. Moving a task earlier does not pull the others along.
  • A red critical path, a baseline you can compare against, and milestones.
  • Weekends and holidays off the calendar.
  • Import from Excel, CSV, or Microsoft Project XML.
  • A PDF report, an Excel export, a PNG, and printing.
  • On Team and Business, a password per person, date-change approvals, a project log, and an audit trail.

A password locks who can sign in. It does not encrypt the file. Keep a confidential plan in a protected folder. GanttOWN also has no iPhone or iPad app, and it does not level resources.
